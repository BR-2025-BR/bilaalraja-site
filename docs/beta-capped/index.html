The beta-capped book

Every buy and sell, December 2013 to July 2026 · point-in-time SEC XBRL · generated 9 September 2026

The six gates, with one addition: a company is only eligible if its own trailing one-year beta is at or below 1.3. That single constraint pulls portfolio beta from 1.34 to 1.05 and holds it there in both halves of the sample.

UNIVERSE US operating companies, financials and real estate excluded
GATES    revenue growth 20–80%, net income > 0, free cash flow > 0,
         ROIC ≥ 10%, FCF conversion ≥ 50%, net debt ≤ 3× EBITDA
BETA     trailing one-year beta ≤ 1.30, measured only on prices before formation
PICK     the 25 highest composite scores that remain
HOLD     one quarter, equal weight, then rebuild

The beta control is the robust part. It delivers 1.05 and 1.04 in the two halves from starting points of 1.17 and 1.38, and volatility falls in both. Sharpe improves sharply in the early period and is unchanged in the late one, so the constraint never costs anything risk-adjusted. The return improvement is not robust — it is +6.2pp in the first half and −5.3pp in the second, which is a sign flip and should be ignored.

Gross of dealing costs, FX, spread and tax. Prices are split- and dividend-adjusted closes; the universe is survivorship-free and delisted names are liquidated at their last traded price. Every fundamental figure is stamped to the date it first became public, and each trailing beta uses only prices before its formation date. The beta screen needs a year of prior prices, so the first two formations of 2014 draw on a thinner eligible set. Fifty-one quarterly formations is a small sample and none of these figures is significance-tested.
