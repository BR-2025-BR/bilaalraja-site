What we fix

One team for every glass and window fault.

If the frame is sound, there is almost never a reason to replace the whole window. We fix the part that failed.

Misted & failed units

Condensation trapped between the panes means the seal has gone. We fit a fresh, energy-efficient sealed unit into your existing frame.

Our speciality

Broken & cracked glass

Cracked, chipped or shattered panes replaced quickly — with a same-day board-up available to keep you secure while we source the glass.

Faulty locks

Seized, snapped or insecure window and door locks replaced to current insurance and anti-snap security standards.

Handles & hinges

Worn handles, dropped hinges and stiff mechanisms replaced so every window and door opens and locks as it should.

Draughts & leaks

Draughty, whistling or rattling frames re-sealed and adjusted — warmer rooms and lower heating bills without a whole new window.

Glass upgrades

While we’re there, upgrade to A-rated energy glass, obscure privacy glass or toughened safety glass — same frame, better performance.

Why it’s cheaper

A new window costs hundreds. A new unit costs tens.

The frame you have is usually fine — it’s the sealed glass or the hardware that fails. Replacing only the failed part typically saves 70–80% against a full window, with no mess, no scaffolding and no fitters in your home for days.
