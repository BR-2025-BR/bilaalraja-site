Yields are the US Treasury daily par yield curve (constant maturity); the policy rate is the effective federal funds rate published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York. Both are pulled at build time — the page itself makes no external calls. The 10y–2y figure is the classic recession-watch spread: negative means the curve is inverted.
